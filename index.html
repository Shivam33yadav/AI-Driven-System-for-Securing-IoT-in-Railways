AI-Driven System for Securing IoT in Railways

A modern metro runs on thousands of connected devices — trains reporting speed and vibration, track sensors watching temperature, signals and gates exchanging commands. This platform watches them all, learns their normal behaviour, and raises the alarm the moment something deviates.

Live Fleet Monitoring

Trains, sensors, signals, cameras and fare gates stream telemetry in real time — speed, vibration, temperature, packet rates and latency.

AI Threat Detection

An anomaly engine learns each device's baseline with rolling z-scores, plus railway rules for floods, spoofed telemetry and MitM-style latency.

Alerts & Response

Threats become severity-ranked alerts with plain explanations. Admins quarantine devices and every action is audit-logged.
